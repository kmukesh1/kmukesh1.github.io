Method

How Algolex reads a career profile

The product is a diagnosis, then a plan. It does not assign you a destiny and it does not apply to jobs for you.

  1. 01

    Profile

    We ask who you are (student, graduate, professional, switcher, returning), what you do now, and the role you want. That changes how experience is scored — a final-year student is not punished for having internships instead of a decade of titles.

  2. 02

    Resume text

    A PDF is read in the browser when possible. You can paste text instead. We look for structure, proof (verbs and numbers), skills, projects, and whether a parser can find ordinary headings.

  3. 03

    Role map

    Your target is matched to a skill map: must-learn, important, nice-to-have. Evidence on the page is compared with that map. Missing skills are not a moral failure; they are a queue.

  4. 04

    Readiness score

    Eight parts roll into one number from 0–100. It is an AI-assisted assessment for guidance. It is not a guarantee of interviews, ATS success, or employment.

  5. 05

    Plan

    A 30/60/90 roadmap, portfolio projects, and interview questions are generated from the gaps — so the next three months are specific instead of “upskill and network.”
