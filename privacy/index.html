Privacy and resume handling

Resumes are personal. Algolex is designed so you can get a diagnosis without turning your CV into a public file. This page describes how the product behaves — it is not a claim of legal compliance with any specific regulation.

What to upload

Use only information you are comfortable submitting for analysis. A resume you would send to a recruiter is the right bar. Do not include government ID numbers, bank details, passwords, or health information.

Where it lives

Parsed text is stored in this browser so you can return to the dashboard. Files are not given public URLs. PDF reading happens on your device when the browser can extract text.

Live analysis

If a live model is available, a truncated copy of the resume text may be sent to a server-side analysis function. API credentials never ship in the frontend. If the model is unavailable, you still receive a guided analysis built from the same role maps.

Limits

PDF or plain text, up to 5 MB. Image-only scans may not extract — paste the text instead.

Delete

Use “Clear this device’s data” in the workspace, or clear site data in your browser. That removes the profile, resume text, and reports stored here.
